About Evans Elite PT

21 years of experience shaped a different approach to physical therapy.

After treating patients in a traditional clinic setting for 21 years, running a physical therapy office, and serving as a company co-director, Brian Evans, DPT, recognized that patients deserved a more personal level of care.

He opened Evans Elite PT to create a practice where patients receive the time, attention, and individualized treatment they need to move forward.

The goal is simple: personalized care, faster progress, and lasting results.

Sessions are held at KORE Bootcamp or KORE Barre & Yoga Studio, or in the comfort of your own home.

How long is a session?

Every visit is a full 60-minute, one-on-one session with your therapist.

What does a session cost?

The rate is $150 per 60-minute session.

What type of treatment do you provide?

Care includes individualized physical therapy, hands-on manual therapy, sports rehabilitation, exercise, and movement guidance based on your goals and history.

Evans Elite PT is a direct pay practice. A superbill can be provided for you to submit to your insurance company for possible reimbursement, depending on your plan.
